hubidubios mobile is the pocket client for a local-first productivity OS. It keeps
a full offline mirror of your vault, syncs only over your own LAN to your own
workstation, and hosts plugin views. No cloud is ever contacted.

- Capture works offline, from a home-screen widget or the share sheet, and
  never fails.
- Tasks, notes, events and the GTD inbox are all available in airplane mode.
- Sync happens automatically when you are home on the LAN; changes are
  field-merged and note conflicts are preserved, never silently discarded.
- Traffic is protected by a pinned self-signed certificate established at
  pairing — stronger than public-CA trust, and unreadable to anything on the
  network.
