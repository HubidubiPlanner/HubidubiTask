HubidubiTask is a task app built around one idea: what you write down should get done, not pile up.

It opens on what is next. Everything new starts from one +. Your phone and your computer (Mac or Linux) keep each other current over your own Wi-Fi. There is no account, no cloud and no server of ours in between.

SEE WHAT IS NEXT
• The app opens on today. Overdue tasks get their own heading at the top, because "should already have happened" calls for a different decision than "happens today".
• Switch to Week or Month when you plan. Weeks roll from today, so on a Saturday "this week" still means seven days.
• Tomorrow never ambushes you: the Day view says how much more is due this week.
• Calendar events sit in the same days as your tasks, with their start time.

ONE + FOR EVERYTHING NEW
• A project, a task, a note, a photo, a picture from the gallery, a voice memo or a file.
• The + is right on Incoming, where the app opens, and in the same place on Projects.
• Hold the + for a note straight away: type it, press Done.
• A task goes into a project or straight to the inbox. A photo, memo or file asks once: something to do, or something to keep?
• Nothing asks for a date: a new task or note is due tomorrow, or next week or undated if you choose that in Settings.
• A New note button for your home screen, and capture from the home-screen widget or any app's share menu.

DONE MEANS DONE
• A long press marks a task done, moves it, dates it or reviews it.
• Projects are plain containers of tasks and notes. Put a note under a task to keep the details where the work is.

WATCH A PAGE
• Add a web page or an RSS or Atom feed. Once a day, week or month it is checked, and when something changed, Claude (always Claude, never your own agent) turns the change into one task in the project you chose.

REVIEW WITH AI (OPTIONAL)
• Long-press any project, task or note and choose Review with AI. It sums up what happened in it, from its tasks and notes, and the summary stays under its title, marked "Not up to date" once something changes.
• Pick the AI in Settings: Claude, from Anthropic, with your own API key; OpenAI, any OpenAI-compatible server you run, with its own model; or Agent, the AI agent you already run on a computer at home (OpenClaw, Hermes, opencode, Kilo, Ollama or any OpenAI-shaped one), by its IP and token. Your agent can look across all your items and add or finish tasks when you ask.
• Reviews go straight from your phone to the AI you chose. Nothing passes through us. Without an AI, everything else works the same.

SYNC WITHOUT A CLOUD
• Pair the phone with the HubidubiTask app on your Mac or Linux computer by typing a short code.
• From then on they keep each other current. Every edit carries its time, the newest wins, and deletions travel too.
• Everything works offline. The phone keeps a full copy in an encrypted database.
• The pairing code can't be stolen on the Wi-Fi, and every sync is sealed with fresh post-quantum keys.

COMING FROM ANOTHER APP?
• One-time import from Todoist (with your API token), Google Calendar (with your private iCal address) or a todo.txt file. The token is used once and not kept.

PRIVACY
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no trackers.
• We run no servers, so we never receive your data.
• The source code is public, so anyone can check this.
• Taking a photo opens your camera app; the microphone is used only while you record a voice memo or say a voice note.

Six colour themes, all high in contrast. The default, Ivory, follows your system's light and dark mode.

In English, Polish, German, French and Spanish. Choose in Settings; until then the app follows your phone's language.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0). Source code: github.com/HubidubiPlanner/HubidubiTask
