• Voice note and Photo note widgets for the home screen.
• Settings › Speech to text: Android writes voice notes down on the phone, or None keeps them as voice memos. The AI you chose tidies the words.
• Settings › Photo to text: the AI writes out the photo, or None keeps the photo.
• Feeds write their task with the AI chosen in Settings.
• Incoming: a long press can move a task to a project, and Date opens on the task's date.
• "No project" now takes a task out of its project.
