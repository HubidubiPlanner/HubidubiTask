Get it on Google Play Mac Linux

  1. Press +

    Pick what it is: a project, a task, a note, a photo, a voice memo or a file. That is the only way in, so there is nothing to remember.

  2. It goes where it belongs

    A task goes into a project, or straight to your inbox. A photo, memo or file asks once: is it something to do, or something to keep?

  3. See what is next

    Next shows today, this week or this month. A long press marks a task done.

  4. Watch a page

    Add a web page as a feed. Once a day, week or month it is checked, and if something changed, AI turns the change into one task.

  5. Ask the AI about it

    Every item can be reviewed by AI: a task, a note, a project. It reads that one item and tells you what is unclear, missing, or the next step.
