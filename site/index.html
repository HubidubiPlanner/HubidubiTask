It opens on what is next

Today first, overdue on its own, then the week or the month. Everything else is one + away.

  1. Press +

    A project, a task, a note, a photo, a voice memo or a file. It is right on Incoming, where the app opens, and it is the only way in. Long-press it instead, and you are writing a note.

  2. It goes where it belongs

    A task goes into a project or your inbox. A photo, memo or file asks once: something to do, or something to keep?

  3. Watch a page

    Add a web page as a feed. Once a day, week or month it is checked, and a change becomes one task.

  4. Review with AI

    Long-press any project, task or note. The AI you chose sums up what happened in it, from its tasks and notes. The summary stays under its title, marked when something has changed since.

Your AI, your choice

Pick one in Settings. Reviews go straight from your device to it. Nothing passes through us.

  • Your model

    OpenAI

    Any server that speaks OpenAI's API, with a model you run yourself.

  • Your key

    Claude

    Anthropic's Claude, with your own API key.

  • Your home

    Agent

    The AI agent you already run at home: OpenClaw, Hermes, opencode or Kilo. It sees all your items, uses its own tools, and adds or finishes tasks when you ask.

Phone and computer, kept in step

Pair them once with a short code. From then on they keep each other current by themselves.

  • Your own Wi-Fi

    The phone and the computer talk to each other directly. Every edit carries its time, and deletions travel too.

  • Yours, offline

    Each device keeps a full copy and works without a connection. On the phone it is an encrypted database.

  • No account

    Nothing to sign up for, and no server of ours. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
