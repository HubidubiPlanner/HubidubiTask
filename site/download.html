Opening the Mac app the first time

HubidubiTask is free and not signed with an Apple developer certificate, so macOS asks you once to confirm that you want to open it. After that it opens like any other app.

  1. Move it to Applications

    Open HubidubiTask.dmg and drag HubidubiTask into the Applications folder.

  2. Open it once

    macOS says it could not verify the app. Click Done.

  3. Allow it in System Settings

    Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Under Security, click Open Anyway next to “HubidubiTask was blocked”, and confirm with your password or Touch ID.

  4. Or, in Terminal

    One line does the same:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/HubidubiTask.app

Installing on Linux

One package with everything it needs. On upgrade it replaces the older hubidubios package, and your items move with it.

  1. Install the package

    sudo pacman -U hubidubitask-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst

  2. Start it

    From the app launcher, or with hubidubitask in a terminal. The package opens the sync port to your home network if ufw is on.

Pair your phone and your computer

Both on the same Wi-Fi. You do this once.

  1. On the computer

    Settings, then Pair a phone. It shows a short code.

  2. On the phone

    Settings, then Pair with a computer. Type the code.

  3. Done

    They keep each other current from now on, over your own Wi-Fi.
