HubidubiOS is a task app built around one idea: what you capture should get done, not pile up.

Your tasks, notes and calendar live on your own devices. Your phone and your computer (Mac or Linux) sync directly with each other over your own Wi-Fi. There is no account, no cloud service and no server of ours in between.

GET IT OUT OF YOUR HEAD
• Capture four ways: type a thought, take or pick a photo, record a voice memo, or attach a document.
• One question when you save: task or note? Only you know whether a photo means "do something about this" or "keep this".
• Add a date right there, while you still remember it, or skip it.
• Capture text from the home-screen widget or from any app's share menu, without opening HubidubiOS.

KNOW WHAT IS NEXT
• Next opens on today. Overdue items get their own heading at the top, because "should already have happened" calls for a different decision than "happens today".
• Switch to Week or Month when you plan. Week shows a seven-day strip with a dot for everything due.
• Weeks roll from today, so on a Saturday "this week" still means seven days, not one.
• Tomorrow never ambushes you: the Day view tells you how much more is due this week.

A CALENDAR THAT BEHAVES LIKE A CALENDAR
• Events sit in the same days as your tasks, with their start time, ahead of the things that can happen any time.
• An event that already happened is not "overdue". It is over, so it leaves the list.
• Events keep the date the calendar gave them; tasks are what you re-date.

DONE MEANS DONE
• Mark an item done from a long-press or from the item itself.
• Projects are plain containers of tasks and notes. Put a note under a task to keep the details where the work is.
• Open any item to see what it is, when it is due, and what is filed under it.

SYNC WITHOUT A CLOUD
• Pair the phone with the HubidubiOS app on your Mac or Linux computer by typing a short code. Both on the same Wi-Fi is all it takes.
• From then on they keep each other current on their own. Every edit carries a time; the newest one wins, and deletions travel too, so nothing you removed comes back.
• Everything works offline. The phone keeps a full copy of your data in an encrypted database.
• Traffic between your devices is encrypted and pinned to the certificate exchanged when you paired.

AN ASSISTANT ON YOUR TERMS (OPTIONAL)
• Ask about your projects, typed or spoken, or ask for a review of a single item.
• The phone talks directly to a model server you run on your own network (any OpenAI-compatible endpoint). Nothing passes through us.
• No server? Everything else works exactly the same.

COMING FROM ANOTHER APP?
• One-time import from Todoist (with your API token) or Google Calendar (with your private iCal address). The token is used once and not kept.

PRIVACY
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no trackers.
• We run no servers, so we never receive your data.
• The camera is used only when you take a photo; the microphone only while you record a voice memo.

Plain paper by day, moss green by night: HubidubiOS follows your system's light and dark theme.
