How it fits together

HubidubiOS Ecosystem

One app for tasks, notes and feeds, on Android, macOS and Linux. A phone and a computer pair once and keep each other current over your own Wi-Fi. Each has a full copy and works on its own. Four AI agents answer questions, review items and turn changed web pages into tasks.

Devices and connections

Solid lines carry your items. Dashed lines are questions to an AI or a feed's look at a page. There is no server of ours anywhere.

AI

Settings offers four agents on every system: Claude, SelfHostedAI, Model and Harness. Any item can be reviewed by the agent, and chat sees a short summary of what is open. Feeds use Claude or the Harness.

Harness requestClaude Code may useWorks in
Chat and reviewsRead, Glob, Grep, WebFetch, subagents, and your items as MCP tools: search, upcoming, projects, project, inbox, add_task, capture, mark_doneA fresh Markdown copy of your items

Always in Claude Code's restricted mode, with only our MCP server and every permission prompt answered no: nothing runs commands or edits files. A harness.env in the app's data folder can point Claude Code at a local model instead of your Claude plan.
